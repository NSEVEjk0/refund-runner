    FROM PROMISE TO PREPARED

    Less chasing.
    More clarity.

    Refund Runner helps when a merchant has already promised your money back. It checks the supplied evidence and gives you a concrete next step.

    01

    Speak, then show the evidence

    Record a short note or type your story. Add the refund confirmation and a bank or card CSV. The caseworker asks for missing evidence.

    02

    Review before calculating

    Check each proposed detail against its source quote. Confirm the statement dates, whether credits are positive or negative, and the original payment promise.

    03

    Check the money, precisely

    A generated program reconciles the rows in an isolated worker. A separate fixed verifier checks the totals and fresh cases with paid, partial, pending and ambiguous credits.

    04

    Practice, approve, take your packet

    The practice desk makes counteroffers. The caseworker refuses store credit and excessive data requests, then pauses on valid terms for your approval. Download a grounded packet to review and use yourself.

    What this first version can establish

    It checks a merchant promise against a supplied statement. It does not authenticate documents, verify transactions outside that statement, decide legal eligibility, connect to your bank, or call real companies. Practice completion is not money recovered.

    Voice notes and extracted confirmation text go to Vultr Serverless Inference after consent. Statement rows stay out of the code-generation prompt and run in networkless workers. The planner sees verified totals for the explanation. Private cases expire after four hours.
