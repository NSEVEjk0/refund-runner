These are fictional demo documents. Northline Outfitters, the purchase, references and transactions are invented. The demo declares statement coverage 2026-10-01 through 2026-11-07 and negative amounts as credits. Demo controls append a posted, pending, partial or ambiguous credit to the same fixture. No real company is contacted.

For personal files, use a text PDF or UTF-8 confirmation and a CSV with `date,description,amount` plus optional `currency,status,reference`. Dates are ISO YYYY-MM-DD. Supported currencies: USD, GBP, EUR. Amounts use at most two decimals. Confirm the credit sign and coverage dates in the app. Missing status defaults to posted and is disclosed. A merchant-only credit without a matching reference is unresolved, not silently assigned to this refund.
